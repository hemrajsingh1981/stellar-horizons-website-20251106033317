Our Missions

Voyage to Kepler-186f

Embark on an interstellar journey to explore the potentially habitable exoplanet Kepler-186f, seeking signs of life and new frontiers for humanity.

Project Chimera: Lunar Base Alpha

Establish the first permanent human settlement on the Moon, Project Chimera, to serve as a research hub and a stepping stone for deeper space exploration.

The Europa Enigma

Dive into the subsurface oceans of Jupiter's moon Europa to investigate its potential to harbor extraterrestrial life, deploying advanced submersible probes.
